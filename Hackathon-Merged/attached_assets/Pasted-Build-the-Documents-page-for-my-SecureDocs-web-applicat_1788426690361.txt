 /documents

Show all legal and investigation documents stored in the system. Users should be able to search, filter, sort, view, upload, download, verify integrity, and manage documents according to their role and permissions.

==================================================

Display integrity as:

⏳ Verification Pending

Do NOT claim that hashing prevents modification.

If Integrity Issue appears, make it visually noticeable and provide a Verify action.

==================================================
21. SECURITY PRINCIPLES
==================================================

The page should reflect three SecureDocs concepts:

PROTECT:
- Role-based access
- Secure document storage
- Authorized downloads

PROVE:
- SHA-256 integrity verification
- Version history
- Audit trail

DETECT:
- Integrity issues
- Suspicious document activity
- Security alerts

Do not falsely claim that SHA-256 itself encrypts or protects the document.
